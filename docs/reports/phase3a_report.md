- **116 teleport legs > 20 km**: ordering errors or genuine long corridors mis-labelled as one line (e.g. `train_24_sncft` tozeur→borj_cedria 370.7 km, les_salines legs ~142 km).
- **238 legs with off-straight deviation > 1000 m**: the snapped road/rail leaves the station-to-station corridor by > 1 km — mostly the teleport legs (les_salines ~29 km, bus_794_18b ~21 km) and a few long rural curves (frachich→sanad ~10.7 km).
